---
title: Schedules
description: Schedule presets, custom expressions, and manual runs.
---

Syncs and recommendations each carry a cron schedule (5 fields: minute, hour, day, month, day of week - e.g. `0 6 * * *`). `TZ` controls the scheduling timezone - refer to [Configuration](/docs/configuration).

## Presets

| Label                  | Expression      |
| ---------------------- | --------------- |
| Every 15 minutes       | `*/15 * * * *`  |
| Every 30 minutes       | `*/30 * * * *`  |
| Every hour             | `0 * * * *`     |
| Every 6 hours          | `0 */6 * * *`   |
| Every 12 hours         | `0 */12 * * *`  |
| Daily at midnight      | `0 0 * * *`     |
| Daily at 6 AM          | `0 6 * * *`     |
| Weekly (Sunday midnight) | `0 0 * * 0`   |

Choose `Simple` for a preset or `Custom` for any valid 5-field expression.

To validate or preview a custom expression, use [crontab.guru](https://crontab.guru/).
